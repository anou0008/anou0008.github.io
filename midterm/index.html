facts about starfish

  • Starfish are not actually fish, they are echinoderms.
  • They have the ability to regenerate lost arms.
  • Starfish have a unique water vascular system that helps them move and capture food.
  • They can have anywhere from 5 to 40 arms, depending on the species.
  • Starfish are found in oceans all over the world, from tropical coral reefs to cold seafloors.
